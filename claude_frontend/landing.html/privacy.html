Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to MEW-Multi Emergent Workspace ("we", "our", "us"). MEW-Multi Emergent Workspace is an autonomous job search and recruiter outreach application designed to streamline job applications, candidate profile management, and career outreach.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and Google user data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you access or use our web application and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our service to you:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, email address, and profile information provided when creating an account or authenticating via Google OAuth.
  • Professional Profile & Application Data: Resume details, work history, skills, job preferences, and saved job descriptions.
  • Authentication & Technical Data: Authentication tokens, browser type, IP address, device information, and usage diagnostics necessary for operating the workspace.

security 3. Google User Data and Gmail Permissions

MEW-Multi Emergent Workspace integrates with Google OAuth to authenticate users and enable recruiter email outreach capabilities. Specifically, our application requests the following Gmail API OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send

Strictly Scoped Usage:

  • We only use Gmail access for draft creation and sending user-approved outreach emails.
  • We use these permissions solely to create recruiter outreach email drafts in the user's Gmail account, and to send recruiter outreach emails only when the user explicitly initiates the send action.
  • We do not read the user's inbox unless a future explicitly listed feature requires it.
  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect strictly to deliver, maintain, and enhance our services, including:

  • Providing automated job matching, resume analysis, and career workflow management.
  • Generating recruiter outreach draft emails directly in your connected Gmail account.
  • Transmitting recruiter outreach emails when you explicitly trigger the action.
  • Responding to user customer service inquiries and support requests.

5. How We Store and Protect Information

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Authentication tokens and user credentials are encrypted both in transit (using TLS/SSL) and at rest.

6. Data Sharing

We respect your data privacy:

  • We do not sell user data or Google user data to any third parties under any circumstances.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties except as strictly necessary to operate our service, comply with legal obligations, or with your explicit consent.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or as required by law.

Account & Data Deletion Request: Users can request the complete deletion of their account and associated data at any time by contacting us directly at akamutala9@gmail.com. Upon receipt of a request, we will delete your stored data within 30 days.
